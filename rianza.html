Rianza — Rules Reference

A tile game of trust and betrayal. Be the first to empty your hand and call DECIO. In the wagering game, the winner also takes the Credit Pot.

Setup

  • Up to 5 players. Shuffle all 28 tiles.
  • Each player takes an equal number of tiles (5 or more). Leftovers form the face-down Boneyard.
  • Each player starts with 20 credits.
  • Keep your tiles hidden — trust no one.

The Tiles

  • Each tile shows two numbers, 0–6.
  • A 0 is wild and may stand for either rolled number.
  • The 0/0 tile may stand for both dice at once.

Playing a Round

Four steps, then play passes to the next player clockwise.

1. Ante & Roll
  • Every player with credits antes 1 into the pot.
  • The roller rolls both dice openly — all claims this round are judged against that roll.
  • Credit Elimination — if only one player can still ante, that player wins the game and takes the pot.
2. Make Claims
  • Each player places one or two tiles face down as a claim — truthfully, as a bluff, or makes no claim at all.
  • No Claim, No Challenge — a player who doesn't claim cannot wager or challenge that round.
  • One tile is truthful if it holds either rolled number (a wild 0 stands for either). Two tiles are truthful if together they cover both numbers.
  • On doubles, every tile in the claim must individually cover the matched number.
Full Match — a single tile that represents both dice at once (roll 3+5 → the 3/5 tile; roll 4+4 → the 4/4 tile; 0/0 is a Full Match for any roll). The strongest possible claim, and the only kind that can win the pot — but only if it's challenged and proves truthful.
3. Wager (wagering variant only)
  • Only players with active claims take part, starting left of the roller — the roller always acts last.
  • Pass 1: check, bet, raise, or fold.
  • Pass 2 (only if needed): players who still owe the bet may call or fold only — no further raising.
  • Wagered credits stay in the pot regardless of outcome. Go all-in if you can't cover a bet in full.
  • Folding returns your claimed tiles to your hand, forfeits any credits already wagered, and takes you out of challenges this round. If everyone folds, the round ends.
4. Challenge
  • Resolved one at a time, starting with the roller and continuing clockwise. Only one player may challenge each claim.
  • No challenge: the claimed tiles go face down into the Boneyard.
  • Truthful, challenged: the challenger takes the claimed tiles, plus the same number of random tiles from the claimant's hand.
  • Bluff, challenged: the claimant keeps the claimed tiles and takes the same number of random tiles from the challenger.
  • Truthful Full Match, challenged: resolves as a truthful claim above — but the claimant also takes the entire pot in credits. A win in credits, a loss in tiles, at the same time.
  • If a player can't provide the full number of tiles owed, they give as many as they have left.
  • After a Full Match wins the pot, every player who still has credits antes 1 to start a new pot.

DECIO!

The moment a player's hand reaches zero tiles, they call "DECIO!" and win immediately — even if other claims from the round haven't been resolved yet. The winner takes the Credit Pot.

Tile-Only Variant (no wagering)

  • Skip credits, the pot, ante, and wager steps — go straight from claims to challenges.
  • Players may pass, but cannot fold.
  • Ignore Full Match pot payouts and credit elimination.
  • All claim, challenge, tile-transfer, Boneyard, and DECIO rules stay the same.

Quick Reference

  • Core rule: no claim, no challenge.
  • Turn order: claims go clockwise from the roller; wagering starts left of the roller (roller last); challenges start at the roller, then clockwise.
  • Challenge results: no challenge → Boneyard. Truthful → challenger takes 2× the claimed tiles. Bluff → claimant takes claim-size tiles from the challenger. Full Match → claimant also takes the pot.
  • Credit elimination: last player who can still ante wins the game and the pot.
  • All-in: can't cover a bet in full? Go all-in with what you have.

Tile ID Notation

S = Series (lower pip, 0–6, printed at the bottom). T = Tile (upper pip, 1–6, printed at the top). Σ = Pip Sum (S+T), shown as the combined grid — not part of the tile's identity.
